# `SFPSTOCHRND`

This instruction has three distinct modes:
* [`SFPSTOCHRND` (Vectorized reduce floating-point precision)](SFPSTOCHRND_FloatFloat.md)
* [`SFPSTOCHRND` (Vectorized convert floating-point to bounded sign-magnitude integer)](SFPSTOCHRND_FloatInt.md)
* [`SFPSTOCHRND` (Vectorized reduce range of sign-magnitude integer)](SFPSTOCHRND_IntInt.md)
